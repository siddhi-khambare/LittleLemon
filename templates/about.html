Our Story

About Little Lemon

Traditional Mediterranean recipes, served with a modern twist since 1995.

Little Lemon

Chicago

Little Lemon began as a small neighbourhood bistro opened by two brothers, Mario and Adrian, who grew up cooking beside their grandmother on the Mediterranean coast. They brought her recipes to Chicago with one promise: honest food made from the freshest ingredients.

Three decades later we are still family-owned. Our menu changes with the seasons, our bread is baked every morning, and every guest is welcomed like part of the family.

Fresh Ingredients

From market to table

Every morning our team visits local Chicago markets and partner farms for vegetables, herbs and citrus. Olive oil, feta and spices are imported from small family producers in Greece and Italy.

  • Locally sourced seasonal vegetables
  • Bread and desserts baked in-house daily
  • Sustainably caught seafood
  • Vegetarian and gluten-free options
